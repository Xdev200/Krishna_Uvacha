Privacy Policy

Last Updated: May 03, 2026

Krishna Uvaach ("we," "our," or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how your information is collected, used, and disclosed by the Krishna Uvaach mobile application.

1. Information Collection

Krishna Uvaach is designed with an "Offline First" philosophy. Most of your data remains on your device.

  • Local Data: Bookmarks, reading history, and app settings are stored locally on your device using SQLite. We do not have access to this data unless you explicitly export it.
  • Usage Data: We may collect anonymous, non-personally identifiable usage statistics to improve the app's performance and user experience.

2. How We Use Information

Any information collected is used solely for:

  • Providing and maintaining the app's features.
  • Improving the "Clinical Luxury" user experience.
  • Analyzing app performance and fixing bugs.

3. Third-Party Services

The app may use third-party services like Google Play Services for app updates and basic analytics. These services have their own privacy policies.

4. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect any data collected. Since most data is stored locally on your device, the security of your data also depends on your device's security settings.

5. Changes to This Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page.

6. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at support@krishnauvaach.app.
